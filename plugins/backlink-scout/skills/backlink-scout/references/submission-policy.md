# Submission policy

## Allowed research and preparation

- Inspect public pages, current submission instructions, visible categories, editorial policies, pricing, and terms.
- Create project-specific candidate reports and factual form copy.
- Prepare a public directory, association, partner, integration, launch, or resource-page submission when it is genuinely relevant.
- Use real company and product facts supplied by the user or verified on the project's public website.
- Record nofollow, sponsored, or unknown link attributes neutrally. A nofollow link can still be useful to people.

## External action gate

Default to `research` or `prepare`. Enter `submit` mode only when the user explicitly asks to submit for the named project or candidate set.

Before preparation or submission, read the project's current `launch-readiness.json`. If its submission gate is not `ready`, continue research if useful but do not publish or submit claims. Resolve public pricing/feature contradictions and smoke-test promised current-build functionality first.

Even in `submit` mode, stop before:

- creating an account when no authorized session exists;
- entering a password, API key, payment method, or private personal data;
- accepting material legal terms or certifying facts not already verified;
- solving or bypassing a CAPTCHA or anti-bot mechanism;
- purchasing a placement, starting a trial, or committing to recurring fees;
- publishing a review or testimonial;
- sending a cold email or direct message.

After a stop, report the exact page, remaining fields, and why user action is required.

## Always reject

- paid links sold for ranking manipulation;
- PBNs, expired-domain networks, or disguised link farms;
- mass forum, profile, guestbook, wiki, or blog-comment links;
- fake accounts, fake locations, fake testimonials, or invented customer proof;
- forced reciprocal-link schemes;
- bulk unsolicited outreach or automated follow-up sequences;
- hidden links, keyword-stuffed anchors, redirects intended to conceal the destination, or malware;
- unrelated directories selected only because a form exists.

## Claims

Never promise or imply guaranteed placement, followed links, indexing, rankings, traffic, citations, or revenue. Report observed facts, the date checked, uncertainty, and the next verification step.
